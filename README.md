# 🎯 Tukka Classifier - English, Maths, GK & Reasoning

Upload screenshots of the questions you got wrong in your SSC/Bank/Railway mocks — Gemini AI reads them, sorts each mistake into the right topic folder, and lets you chat with it about your own logged mistakes. Four trackers in one app: English, Maths, GK, and Reasoning.

🔒 Your data stays on your own device by default. No login, no account, no data collection, nothing uploaded anywhere — unless you choose to turn on Google Drive backup, in which case your data goes only to your own personal Google Drive (never to me or anyone else).

📱 Install as an app (Android): Download the APK from the [Releases](https://claude.ai/releases) section. (Select Tukka Classifier APK.)

🔗 Use it now (no install needed): [your-username.github.io/tukka-classifier](https://your-username.github.io/tukka-classifier/)

The app may ask for two permissions, both fully optional and never automatic: importing a JSON backup file, or signing into your own Google account if you turn on Drive sync. It also needs a Gemini API key (free from Google AI Studio) to classify your screenshots and power the AI chat — the app itself never asks for or stores any key of its own.

⚠️ Note on installing the APK: since this isn't from the Google Play Store, Android will show a warning like "Install blocked" or "Unknown app installed for your security" when you first try to install it. This is normal for any APK shared outside the Play Store — just tap "Install anyway" or enable "Allow from this source" when prompted. The app itself is safe; this warning appears for any APK not downloaded from the Play Store.

## What it does

- Four trackers in one app — English, Maths, GK, and Reasoning — switch between them anytime
- Upload screenshots or a short screen recording of the mistakes from your mock; Gemini automatically sorts each one into the right topic folder:
  - **English** — Vocabulary, Grammar & Reading
  - **Maths** — 30 topics, with formulas, equations & tables rendered properly
  - **GK** — Geography, History, Polity, Economics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Environment & Static GK
  - **Reasoning** — Analogy, Series, Puzzles, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Seating & more
- AI chat — ask Gemini questions about your own logged mistakes ("Which folders keep costing me marks?") using your own free API key
- Backup & Restore — export all your logged mistakes to a file, or import them back anytime
- Works fully offline for browsing and reviewing your logged mistakes; an internet connection is only needed when uploading new screenshots or using the AI chat

## Why it's safe to use

- No sign-up, no account, no personal information asked
- Your data lives only on your own browser/phone
- Screenshots you upload go straight from your device to Google's Gemini API using your own personal API key — never through any server of mine
- The only other time this app connects to the internet is if you turn on Google Drive sync — off by default, entirely your choice, and your data goes straight to your own Drive account, not through any server of mine

## How to use

1. Pick a tracker — English, Maths, GK, or Reasoning.
2. Add your free Gemini API key from Google AI Studio (one-time setup, shared across all four trackers).
3. Upload screenshots or a screen recording of the mistakes from your latest mock.
4. Let Gemini sort them into topic folders, then browse or ask the AI chat about any of them.
5. Switch between English, Maths, GK, and Reasoning trackers anytime.
